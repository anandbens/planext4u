# 04 — Ecommerce

Services involved: `catalog-svc`, `orders-svc`, `payments-svc`, `promotions-svc`, `identity-svc` (wallet), `vendor-svc`, `search-svc`, `logistics-svc`.

## 1. Catalog

### 1.1 Hierarchy

```text
country → category (self-referencing tree, category_type) → parent_item (brand/model grouping)
        → product → product_variant → variant images
                  → attribute values (via product_attribute_map)
```

- **Categories** are a self-referencing tree with `parent_id`, ordered by `display_order`, flagged for homepage visibility, trending, emergency, and carrying theme colours, banner/promotion fields and a category-level commission rate.
- **Parent items** group products that are the same model across vendors, enabling brand/model rollups and price comparison.
- **Attributes** are hierarchical and cascade: selecting a value narrows the next attribute's options. Apparel categories **must** require Colour and Size; the requirement is data-driven per category, not hardcoded.
- **Variants** carry their own SKU, price, MRP, stock and images. A product with variants sells only through variants; a product without variants sells directly.

### 1.2 Product lifecycle

`draft → submitted → approved → active ⇄ paused → archived`, with `rejected` reachable from `submitted` carrying a mandatory reason. Only `approved`+`active` products are visible to customers or indexed for search. Vendor edits to price and stock apply immediately; edits to title, images, category or attributes on an approved product return it to `submitted` for re-approval (configurable per field set).

### 1.3 Required fields

Title, description (rich text — a plain textarea is not acceptable for multi-line content fields), category, at least one image, price, MRP, tax class (HSN code, GST rate), stock policy, weight/dimensions for shippable goods, country, and vendor. SEO fields (slug, meta title, meta description) are required and may be generated by the AI assistant (`15-ai-services.md`).

### 1.4 Inventory

An append-only inventory ledger records every movement with reason (`purchase`, `sale`, `return`, `adjustment`, `damage`, `reservation`, `release`). Current stock is the ledger sum, cached in the product/variant row and reconciled by a scheduled job. Deduction at order confirmation is atomic and must fail the order rather than allow negative stock, unless the product is flagged backorderable. Low-stock thresholds raise vendor notifications.

### 1.5 Media

Images WebP, quality 70, max 2048 px on the long edge; video H.264, 480p, max 45 s. Uploads go through `media-workers` (see `14`). Only real photography — placeholder or generated stand-in imagery is not acceptable in production data.

### 1.6 Reviews

A review may be written only by an account that has a `delivered` or `completed` order containing that product, one review per order line, editable for 7 days. Reviews carry rating (1–5), text, optional images, helpful votes, and a moderation state. Aggregates (average, count, distribution) are maintained by event consumers, not computed per request.

## 2. Discovery

- **Browse** — category, subcategory, vendor, price range, attribute facets, rating, availability, delivery speed, and distance from the customer's active coordinates.
- **Location filtering** — products are filtered by Haversine distance from the customer's selected coordinates against the vendor's serviceable radius; in the target build this is a PostGIS `ST_DWithin` query against the vendor service-area geometry (`14`).
- **Autocomplete** — live suggestions spanning categories, products, services and vendors, debounced, ranked by exact prefix, then popularity, then distance.
- **Sorting** — relevance, price asc/desc, newest, rating, distance, discount.
- Search behaviour, ranking and index definitions are specified in `14-search-media-geo.md`.

## 3. Cart

- Server-authoritative. The client never computes totals it can submit.
- Multi-vendor carts split into per-vendor shipment groups at checkout; each group carries its own delivery fee and settlement attribution.
- On every cart read the server revalidates: product still active, variant still exists, stock available, price unchanged (a price change surfaces an explicit "price updated" notice), and vendor still serviceable at the delivery address.
- Cart persists per account and merges a guest-browsing cart on login.

## 4. Pricing and totals

Computed exclusively server-side, in this order:

```text
1. line_subtotal      = Σ (unit_price × qty)
2. line_discounts     = automatic cart rules (promotions-svc)
3. coupon_discount    = user-entered code (promotions-svc, validated & reserved)
4. taxable_value      = line_subtotal − line_discounts − coupon_discount
5. tax                = per-line GST by HSN/tax class, CGST/SGST or IGST by place of supply
6. delivery_fee       = per shipment group (zone rules, distance, surge, free-delivery threshold)
7. platform_fee       = configurable, per country/module
8. grand_total_before_points
9. points_redeemed    = min(user_request, max_redeemable)   ← see §6.3
10. amount_payable    = grand_total_before_points − points_redeemed
```

Every component must be itemised in the response and displayed in the checkout summary — the points arithmetic in particular must be shown explicitly so the customer can see how the final amount was reached. Rounding is applied once, at the final payable amount, to two decimals, half-up.

## 5. Coupons and cart rules

Two distinct mechanisms:

| | Cart rule | Coupon code |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Automatic when conditions match | Customer enters a code |
| Visibility | Implicit | Explicit, listed on the coupons screen |
| Storage | `cart_rules` | `coupon_codes` under `coupon_campaigns` |
| Stacking | Governed by `stackable` + `priority` | Governed by campaign rules |

### 5.1 Coupon domain

Campaign → codes (single-use, multi-use, or bulk-generated) → targeting maps: product, category, vendor, customer, geography. Constraints: min/max cart value, module scope, country scope, start/end window, total-use cap, per-customer cap, first-order-only, new-user-only, payment-method restriction.

### 5.2 Reservation protocol

Redemption is two-phase to prevent over-issue under concurrency:
1. **Reserve** at checkout start — a reservation row with a TTL (default 15 min) decrements available usage atomically.
2. **Commit** on payment success — reservation becomes a redemption; usage counters and analytics update.
3. **Release** on payment failure, cart abandonment or TTL expiry — a scheduled reaper releases stale reservations.
4. **Rollback** — an order cancelled or refunded after commit produces a rollback record restoring the customer's usage allowance according to the campaign's rollback policy.

### 5.3 Discount bearer

Every discount records who pays for it: `platform`, `vendor`, or a split with an explicit breakup. The bearer determines settlement: a vendor-borne discount reduces the vendor's payable; a platform-borne discount does not. The bearer breakup is snapshotted onto the order at application time so later configuration changes cannot rewrite history.

### 5.4 Coupon abuse controls

Every redemption attempt is evaluated by the fraud rules engine: device fingerprint reuse across accounts, velocity, referral self-dealing, and blacklisted identifiers. Outcomes are recorded with the matched rule. Analytics and audit logs record every issue, redemption, rollback and configuration change.

### 5.5 Popup campaigns

Geo-targeted popup offers are shown to eligible users (radius from configured coordinates, new-user or segment filters), with per-user dismissal tracking so a dismissed popup does not reappear within its configured cooldown.

## 6. Wallet, cashback and referrals

### 6.1 Points ledger

The wallet is an **append-only ledger**; balance is never stored as a mutable scalar of record. Entry types: `earn_order`, `earn_cashback`, `earn_referral`, `earn_bonus`, `redeem`, `expire`, `reversal`, `adjustment`. 1 point = ₹1. Each earn entry carries an expiry date; consumption is **FIFO by expiry**. A nightly job writes `expire` entries for lapsed balances and notifies the user in advance.

### 6.2 Earning

| Trigger | Rule |
|---|---|
| Order completion | Configurable % of net paid value, credited after the return window closes |
| Cashback campaign | Campaign-defined amount or %, capped per order and per user |
| Referral — referrer | Credited when the referee completes their first qualifying order |
| Referral — referee | Credited on registration with the referral code, released on first order |
| Bonus workflows | e.g. automated 2% bonus on qualifying completions, configurable |

### 6.3 Redemption — maximum redeemable points

This is a hard correctness requirement and a known defect class.

```text
max_redeemable = floor( min(
      wallet_available_balance,
      grand_total_before_points × max_redemption_percent,
      max_redemption_absolute_cap
) )
```
`max_redemption_percent` and `max_redemption_absolute_cap` are platform configuration, overridable per campaign or per vendor plan. Requirements:

1. The input control must **not permit** a value greater than `max_redeemable` — the value is clamped as the user types, non-numeric input is rejected, and leading zeros and decimals are normalised.
2. The server independently recomputes `max_redeemable` and rejects any request exceeding it with a specific error; the client clamp is a convenience, never the control.
3. The computed maximum, the entered value and the resulting reduction are all displayed in the cost summary.
4. Partial redemption is always allowed; full redemption to a zero payable is allowed only when the platform permits points-only orders.

### 6.4 Reversal

Refunds reverse points symmetrically: redeemed points are credited back with their original expiry preserved; earned points on the refunded value are debited. If the earned points have already been spent, the debit may drive the balance negative up to a configured floor, or be written off, per configuration.

## 7. Checkout and orders

### 7.1 Checkout saga

`orders-svc` orchestrates (see `01 §4.3`): validate cart → reserve stock → reserve coupon → compute totals → create order in `pending_payment` → authorize payment → debit points → confirm order → emit `order.confirmed`. Any failure runs the compensations in reverse. The whole saga is idempotent on the client's `Idempotency-Key`.

### 7.2 Order model

An order has header (customer, addresses, country, currency, totals snapshot, discount snapshot, tax snapshot, payment reference) and lines (vendor, product, variant, quantity, unit price, per-line discount and tax, per-line status). A multi-vendor order produces one shipment group per vendor, each independently fulfilled and settled, while the customer sees one order.

### 7.3 Status lifecycle

```text
pending_payment → confirmed → processing → packed → shipped → out_for_delivery → delivered → completed
                     ↘ cancelled            ↘ cancelled        ↘ returned → refunded
```
Every transition writes a status-history row (actor, role, timestamp, note) and emits an event. Allowed transitions are declared in a state machine; an illegal transition is a `409`, never a silent no-op.

### 7.4 Delivery scheduling

Two modes: **Anytime** and **Scheduled** with a date plus a slot (Morning / Afternoon / Evening). Slot availability is computed from vendor capacity, zone rules and cut-off times. The chosen slot is snapshotted on the order and drives dispatch prioritisation.

### 7.5 Proof of delivery

Delivery cannot be marked complete without proof capture: photo, recipient name, and either OTP confirmation or signature, per configuration. The proof is uploaded before the status transition is accepted; the transition is rejected if the proof is missing.

### 7.6 Returns and refunds

Return window is configurable per category. A return request captures reason, evidence images and pickup address; approval triggers a reverse assignment; receipt triggers refund. Refunds go back to the original payment instrument by default, with wallet credit as an alternative. A credit note is issued for every refund of an invoiced order.

## 8. Invoicing and tax

- Invoices are generated at a country-configured trigger point (typically on confirmation or on shipment) from `payments-svc`, using an atomic per-country, per-financial-year sequence — gaps and duplicates are both defects.
- GST: place-of-supply determines CGST+SGST versus IGST; per-line HSN and rate are snapshotted from the tax class at order time.
- Credit notes reference the original invoice and carry their own sequence.
- Statutory outputs are specified in `10-admin-and-reporting.md`.

## 9. Vendor commission

Commission is resolved by a strict cascade, first match wins:

```text
1. product-level commission override
2. vendor-level commission override
3. vendor plan commission
4. category commission rate
5. platform default commission
```
The resolved rate and its source are snapshotted onto the order line so settlement is reproducible and auditable.

## 10. Dropshipping

Suppliers, supplier products and per-vendor dropshipping settings allow a vendor to list a supplier's catalogue. A dropshipping order records the supplier, the supplier's own reference, and the margin split. Fulfilment status syncs from the supplier and maps into the standard order lifecycle.

## 11. Wishlist

Segregated into Products, Services and Sellers, each with its own list and its own "notify me" behaviour (price drop, back in stock, new listing from a followed seller).

## 12. Acceptance criteria

1. Totals returned by the server are byte-identical to totals recomputed independently from the order snapshot.
2. Concurrent checkout of the last unit of stock results in exactly one confirmed order.
3. A coupon with a total-use cap of *N* can never be committed more than *N* times under concurrent load.
4. Points redemption above `max_redeemable` is impossible from the UI and rejected by the API.
5. Cancelling a paid order restores stock, releases the coupon, reverses points and issues a refund plus credit note, with all five verifiable from the order's event history.
6. A multi-vendor order settles each vendor independently at the correct commission, with discount bearers respected.
